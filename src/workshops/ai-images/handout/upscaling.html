Make it bigger

An upscale model makes a picture 4 times wider and taller in under a minute. It sharpens what's there; it can't invent new detail. We use Remacri: it keeps soft detail soft instead of turning it into harsh texture.

The easy way: open a ready-made workflow

  1. Copy 4x_foolhardy_Remacri.safetensors from the stick's models/upscale_models into ComfyUI's upscale_models folder (same place as page 5 of the main sheet).
  2. Drag upscale-4x.json or upscale-8x.json from the stick's workflows folder onto ComfyUI.
  3. On box 1. Your picture, click choose file to upload and pick your picture.
  4. Click Run. The result appears in the Save box with its new size underneath.

4x or 8x?

WorkflowWhat it doesA 2135 × 736 picture becomes
upscale-4xRemacri8,540 × 2,944
upscale-8xRemacri, then Upscale Image By 2 (a plain resize)17,080 × 5,888

Don't run Remacri twice: that's 16 times (4 × 4), far too big for most laptops.

How big do I need?For a print, divide the pixels by the inches. 5,888 pixels on a wall 1 metre (39 inches) high is about 150 pixels per inch: fine for something you look at from a step back. Photos you hold in your hand want about 300.

Build it yourself

Four boxes, all built into ComfyUI:

Load Image→Load Upscale Model→Upscale Image (using Model)→Save Image
  1. Click + next to the tabs at the top for a new, empty workflow.
  2. Double-click empty canvas, type Load Image, press Enter. Choose your picture.
  3. Double-click, add Load Upscale Model. Choose 4x_foolhardy_Remacri.safetensors.
  4. Double-click, add Upscale Image (using Model). Wire UPSCALE_MODEL and IMAGE into it.
  5. Double-click, add Save Image. Wire the upscaled IMAGE into it. Click Run.
  6. For 8x: put an Upscale Image By box (method lanczos, scale 2) between the upscale and Save.

We compared five ways to make it 8x bigger

MethodResult
Plain resizeSoft and blurry, but makes sense
4x-UltraSharpSharp, but fine detail turns into harsh stripes
4x-UltraSharp V2Everything looks like hammered metal
Real-ESRGANClean, smoothed into a painting
RemacriBest: soft detail stays soft, edges stay sharp

The whole 8x picture (17,080 × 5,888) took 60 seconds on a 16 GB M1 MacBook.
